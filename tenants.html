Please pay rent directly to your owner, using the method in your lease. We do not collect rent. Questions? Contact us.

When a repair needs someone to enter your home, we will give you at least 24 hours' written notice, except in an emergency.

Give written notice as your lease requires. Clean the home, return all keys and remotes, and share a forwarding address.

Your deposit is held by your owner. After move-out, it is returned with an itemized statement of any deductions within 21 days, as California law requires.

The owner's insurance does not cover your belongings. We recommend renter's insurance, and many leases require it.

Quick Fixes

Try this first

Many small problems can be solved in a minute. If these steps don't work, send us a service request.

Part of my home has no power

Check the electrical panel for a breaker in the middle or "off" position. Push it fully off, then back on. In kitchens, bathrooms and outdoors, also press "Reset" on the GFCI outlet (the one with two small buttons).

My garbage disposal hums but doesn't turn

Turn it off. Press the small red reset button on the bottom of the unit. If it is still stuck, use the hex key in the slot at the bottom center to turn it back and forth. Never put your hand inside.

The toilet is clogged or keeps running

For a clog, use a plunger with steady pushes. If water is rising, turn the valve behind the toilet clockwise to stop it. For a running toilet, check that the chain inside the tank is not tangled and the flapper closes.

There is no hot water

For a gas water heater, check that the pilot light is on and follow the relight steps on the label. For an electric one, check the breaker. If you smell gas, leave the home and call the gas company and 911.

The smoke alarm keeps chirping

A short chirp every minute usually means a low battery. Replace the battery and press the test button. Never remove an alarm without replacing it — working alarms are required.

The heater or AC is not working

Check that the thermostat is set to Heat or Cool and has fresh batteries. Check the breaker, and make sure the air filter is not blocked. If it still doesn't work, send a request.

Bill Qin & the PM Team

Bill Qin came to the U.S. 20 years ago and knows how important it is to find the right home — one that feels safe, warm and relaxing.

With more than 15 years in Bay Area real estate, Bill and his team now help owners lease and care for their homes, with fast responses, clear communication and a reliable network of local service providers.

Leasing, management and sales live under one roof — so owners who later want to sell, buy or invest work with the same team that already knows their home.
